Guardian Spirits and the Restoration of Teradea

How Guardian Spirits, village aid, Happilia, disasters, and the forest mystery fit the confirmed story framework.

Dossier summary

The player helps Guardian Spirits revive and revitalize Teradea while assisting villages affected by storms, earthquakes, fading communities, and the spreading mist. Happilia rewards contributions to villagers and development.

Confirmed story role

01

Guardian Spirits are described as powerful beings the player can befriend.

02

The Harvest Goddess guides the player and friends in each village provide support.

03

Village aid and personal relationships are both positioned as parts of the restoration journey.

Launch update boundary

This framework supports later individual Spirit, village-project, and Happilia guides once names, requirements, abilities, and rewards are verified.

Known boundary

The number, names, locations, forms, quests, and powers of the Guardian Spirits have not been published.
