From Bloomfield Village to the Wider Teradea Journey

How the player’s home village, nighttime wolves, best friend, and Guardian Spirit mission launch the wider story.

Dossier summary

The player is raised in Bloomfield Village. When mist spreads from the Forest of Echoes and wolves appear at night, the player and a best friend set out to tame them, opening a larger mission to help Guardian Spirits restore Teradea.

Confirmed story role

01

Bloomfield is the player’s home base and the place to which wilderness failure returns them.

02

Lupo is described in the preorder campaign as the Bloomfield Guardian.

03

The story expands from a local wolf problem into village disasters and the mystery behind the mist.

Launch update boundary

This framing distinguishes the confirmed premise from later quest structure, dialogue, character identity, and reward details that have not been released.

Known boundary

The best friend’s name, opening quest steps, Bloomfield residents, shops, map, and daily schedule are not public.
