Official Sources for Echoes of Teradea Information

A source map explaining which official page is best for story, gameplay, release, preorder, retailer, price, and policy details.

Reference answer

Use the March announcement for the original world and feature reveal, the May release for date and preorder evidence, the June release for trailer-era confirmation and retailer additions, and the Natsume Store for current price, physical variants, player count, rating status, shipping, and region policy.

Source responsibilities

01

Press releases are stable dated evidence for what was known at a milestone.

02

The product page is more suitable for live commercial details that may change.

03

Platform storefronts should become the primary source for platform-specific technical fields once official listings appear.

Best use

A reliable page can cite more than one source when claims come from different evidence layers; it should never cite one release as proof of a detail that only appears elsewhere.

Known boundary

This fan guide is independent and does not replace Natsume’s support, retailer terms, or final platform listings.

Why this page is useful before launch

This page resolves a distinct planning or fact-checking intent with dated first-party evidence. It does not create character names, item values, quest steps, map coordinates, or feature claims from series precedent. Verified launch data can deepen the answer without changing its search intent.
