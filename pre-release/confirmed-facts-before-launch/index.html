What Is Confirmed About Echoes of Teradea Before Launch?

The concise confirmed-facts sheet for the release date, platforms, player count, genre, places, characters, and gameplay systems.

Direct answer

Confirmed facts include a September 24, 2026 launch, Switch 2, Switch, PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Steam versions, a one-player Simulation/Farming classification, ten love interests, and an open world built around farming, exploration, animal companions, campsites, villages, caves, and islands.

Evidence inventory

01

Named characters or figures are the Harvest Goddess, Doc Jr., Lupo, Guardian Spirits, and a rumored guardian wolf.

03

Power Statues free dormant Power Wisps; Power Wisp Fruits can be traded at the Forest Goddess Statue for stamina increases and useful abilities.
