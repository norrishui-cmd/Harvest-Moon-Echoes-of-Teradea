Campsite to Traveling Merchant Workflow

Campsites support rest, stamina recovery, and cooking during long trips, while traveling merchants offer goods unavailable elsewhere. Together they form a travel-resupply workflow, though schedules and locations are not yet confirmed.

Direct answer

Campsites support rest, stamina recovery, and cooking during long trips, while traveling merchants offer goods unavailable elsewhere. Together they form a travel-resupply workflow, though schedules and locations are not yet confirmed.

Workflow

  1. Reach or use a verified campsite.
  2. Check nearby travel routes for a merchant encounter.
  3. Record item, price, day, weather, and repeatability after launch.

Confirmed system anchors

  • The world is described as too large to explore in one in-game day.
  • Camping extends expeditions through rest and cooking.
  • Traveling merchants are associated with exclusive items.
