Wildlife Risk and Recovery Loop

Dangerous wild animals appear at night and can catch the player. Natsume says the penalty can include losing collected items and being returned to the farm, so route planning should account for time, escape, and carried value.

Direct answer

Dangerous wild animals appear at night and can catch the player. Natsume says the penalty can include losing collected items and being returned to the farm, so route planning should account for time, escape, and carried value.

Workflow

  1. Track time and the nearby safe route.
  2. Record the animal and encounter trigger.
  3. Verify which items are lost and where recovery begins.

Confirmed system anchors

  • Night wolves are part of Bloomfield’s opening threat.
  • The wilderness includes bears, tigers, and other dangerous animals.
  • Combat, stealth, taming conditions, exact loss rules, and difficulty settings are not fully described.
