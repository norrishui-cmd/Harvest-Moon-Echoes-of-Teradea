Gameplay System Workflows

Each workflow follows a different player goal and maps only the links Natsume has described. Exact unlock steps, values, and coordinates remain reserved for verified launch data.

Animal Obstacle Workflow

Animal Companion Obstacle Workflow

Confirmed animal abilities support a clear exploration loop: identify a blocked route, select a companion suited to movement or destruction, pass the obstacle, and search the newly reachable area for treasure or other discoveries.

Hidden Area Discovery Loop

Hidden Area Discovery Loop

Hidden-area discovery combines player movement, animal traversal abilities, obstacle removal, and careful route observation. The confirmed systems support the loop even though exact locations are not public.

Camp To Merchant Workflow

Campsite to Traveling Merchant Workflow

Campsites support rest, stamina recovery, and cooking during long trips, while traveling merchants offer goods unavailable elsewhere. Together they form a travel-resupply workflow, though schedules and locations are not yet confirmed.

Camp Stamina Cooking Loop

Camp, Stamina, and Cooking Loop

During extended exploration, campsites let players rest, recover stamina, and cook by the fire. The practical loop is travel, monitor stamina, camp before exhaustion, prepare food, and continue the expedition.

Nautical Chart Island Loop

Nautical Chart to Remote Island Workflow

Nautical charts lead to remote islands, where players can hunt treasure and encounter rare animals not found on the mainland. That creates a chart, travel, search, befriend, and return loop.

Cave Ore Gem Loop

Cave, Ore, and Gem Expedition Loop

Maze-like caves contain ore and gems, making cave exploration a distinct resource expedition. A reliable loop will connect route entry, stamina preparation, navigation, extraction, inventory choices, and safe return.

Power Wisp Progression Loop

Power Wisp Progression Loop

The confirmed progression chain is to solve Power Statue puzzles, release dormant Power Wisps, receive Power Wisp Fruits, and trade those fruits at the Forest Goddess Statue for stamina increases and useful abilities.

Happilia Restoration Loop

Happilia and Village Restoration Loop

Helping villagers and contributing to Teradea’s development generates Happilia. That places community assistance inside the wider goal of reviving villages and restoring the land.

Farm To Expedition Planning

Farm to Expedition Planning Loop

Echoes of Teradea combines running a farm with journeys through villages, caves, wilderness, and islands. The planning loop is to prepare at the farm, choose a goal and companion, travel, gather or help, then return with verified resources and progress.

Wildlife Risk Recovery Loop

Wildlife Risk and Recovery Loop

Dangerous wild animals appear at night and can catch the player. Natsume says the penalty can include losing collected items and being returned to the farm, so route planning should account for time, escape, and carried value.

Treasure Collectible Planning

Treasure and Rare Collectible Planning

Treasures, rare collectibles, caves, islands, animal abilities, and obstacle removal form a discovery network. A useful hunt starts with a verified access requirement and ends with a reproducible location and reward.

Village Travel Priority Loop

Four-Village Travel Priority Framework

Bloomfield is the player’s home, Tidewind faces storms, Quarrytop faces earthquakes, and Maplehill needs renewed community life. The game’s exact quest order is unknown, so the safest framework organizes travel by confirmed village purpose rather than claiming a route.
