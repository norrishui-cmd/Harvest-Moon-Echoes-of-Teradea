Power Wisp Progression Loop

The confirmed progression chain is to solve Power Statue puzzles, release dormant Power Wisps, receive Power Wisp Fruits, and trade those fruits at the Forest Goddess Statue for stamina increases and useful abilities.

Direct answer

The confirmed progression chain is to solve Power Statue puzzles, release dormant Power Wisps, receive Power Wisp Fruits, and trade those fruits at the Forest Goddess Statue for stamina increases and useful abilities.

Workflow

  1. Find and verify a statue.
  2. Document the puzzle and released Wisp.
  3. Record the fruit reward and confirmed exchange result.

Confirmed system anchors

  • Power Statues are puzzle-linked progression objects.
  • Power Wisp Fruits connect rescue activity to character growth.
  • Costs, ability names, puzzle solutions, and statue locations are not published.
