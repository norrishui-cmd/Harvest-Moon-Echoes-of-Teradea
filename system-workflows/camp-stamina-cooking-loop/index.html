Camp, Stamina, and Cooking Loop

During extended exploration, campsites let players rest, recover stamina, and cook by the fire. The practical loop is travel, monitor stamina, camp before exhaustion, prepare food, and continue the expedition.

Direct answer

During extended exploration, campsites let players rest, recover stamina, and cook by the fire. The practical loop is travel, monitor stamina, camp before exhaustion, prepare food, and continue the expedition.

Workflow

  1. Carry verified cooking materials after launch.
  2. Use stamina thresholds only when measured.
  3. Record whether rest advances time and how much it restores.

Confirmed system anchors

  • Camping is explicitly tied to long-distance exploration.
  • Rest and food both belong to the campsite description.
  • No recovery values, recipe list, placement rules, or time cost are officially published.
