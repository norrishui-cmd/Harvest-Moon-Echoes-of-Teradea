Fact-checked guides for treasure hunting, mining, merchants, stamina, rare animals, pets and mounts.

Echoes of Teradea Treasure Hunting Guide

Treasure hunting is confirmed in caves, hidden areas and remote islands. Nautical charts reveal islands, while animal abilities and expanded movement help players reach secrets.

Echoes of Teradea Mining Caves, Ore, and Gems

Natsume confirms maze-like caves containing ore and gems. Quarrytop is also a named mining village, but exact mine floors, ore tables and tool requirements are not yet public.

Echoes of Teradea Traveling Merchants Guide

Traveling merchants appear at campsites and sell exclusive items that are not available elsewhere. Their names, routes, schedules and inventories have not yet been disclosed.

Echoes of Teradea Stamina Recovery and Upgrades

Players can recover stamina by resting at campsites. Power Wisp Fruits can also be exchanged at the Forest Goddess Statue to boost stamina and unlock useful abilities.

Echoes of Teradea Rare Animals and Island Wildlife

Rare animals can be found on remote islands, including animals that do not appear on the mainland. Players use nautical charts to locate those islands and can befriend the animals there.

Echoes of Teradea Pets, Animals, and Mounts Explained

Pets, animals and mounts can accompany the player, and each companion or mount has special abilities that help explore uncharted territory. The official material does not yet provide a full category-by-category roster.

Nautical Charts in Echoes of Teradea

Nautical Charts are confirmed exploration items used to locate remote islands where players can hunt for treasure and befriend rare animals not found on the mainland.

Power Wisp Fruits in Echoes of Teradea

Power Wisp Fruits are rewards from Power Statue challenges. Players trade them at the Forest Goddess Statue to increase stamina and unlock useful abilities.

Ore and Gems in Echoes of Teradea

Ore and gems are confirmed resources found in maze-like caves across Teradea. Quarrytop is the named mining village, but the official material does not place every mining resource there.

Exploration Ability Combinations in Echoes of Teradea

Exploration combines player movement—jumping, ladders, and vines—with animal abilities that cross terrain, destroy rocks or fallen trees, reach hidden areas, and uncover treasure.

Village Restoration and Happilia Gameplay Loop

Players earn Happilia by helping villagers and contributing to Teradea’s development while investigating each settlement’s crisis and rebuilding the wider world.

How Do You Avoid Losing Collected Items in the Wilderness?

Natsume says players must evade or escape wolves, bears and tigers; being caught causes collected items to be lost and sends the player back to the farm.

What Can Animal Companions Help You Do?

Animal companions can cross terrain, break rocks and fallen trees, reach hidden areas and uncover valuable treasures.

Can You Cook and Sleep from the Camp Interface?

Yes. A current interface screenshot visibly includes both “Cook” and “Sleep” commands, matching Natsume’s campsite description.

How Do You Open Photo Mode?

A current screenshot shows Photo Mode as a directly mapped interface command, although final platform-specific controls have not been documented.

How Do You Find the Spirit Tree at Wolf Hill?

A current screenshot confirms Wolf Hill as the objective destination, but it does not show a complete route or map coordinates.

Can Campsites Support Longer Exploration Trips?

Yes. Natsume describes campsites as places to cook, sleep, and recover, making them an official support system for trips away from the farm.

Yes. Official descriptions say animals have special abilities that help the player leap across terrain, break obstacles, and uncover treasures.
