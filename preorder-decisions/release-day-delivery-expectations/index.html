Release-Day Delivery Expectations

September 24, 2026 is the game’s release date, but the Natsume Store does not guarantee that a physical preorder arrives that day. Release timing and parcel delivery are separate events.

Direct answer

September 24, 2026 is the game’s release date, but the Natsume Store does not guarantee that a physical preorder arrives that day. Release timing and parcel delivery are separate events.

Workflow

  1. Use the confirmed date for the game.
  2. Use tracking for the parcel.
  3. Check a platform storefront separately for digital availability.

Confirmed system anchors

  • Carrier transit is unpredictable and release-day delivery is not guaranteed.
  • Digital unlock timing is also still unpublished.
