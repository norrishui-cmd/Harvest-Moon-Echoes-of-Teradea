Retailer and Platform Preorder Checklist

Natsume has named its store, Amazon, Target, Walmart, Best Buy, and GameStop, but a named retailer does not guarantee every platform, permanent stock, or identical bonus terms.

Direct answer

Natsume has named its store, Amazon, Target, Walmart, Best Buy, and GameStop, but a named retailer does not guarantee every platform, permanent stock, or identical bonus terms.

Verification checklist

  1. Confirm seller and platform.
  2. Compare bonus and cancellation language.
  3. Record delivery or pickup estimate before paying.

Evidence behind the checklist

  • Amazon was named with Switch 2, Switch, and PS5 when preorders opened.
  • Later material named additional major retailers.
  • Live seller identity, stock, pickup, cancellation, and bonus wording can differ.
