Lupo Bonus Verification

How to Verify the Lupo Plush Bonus

A qualifying listing must explicitly show the Lupo baby wolf plush offer for the selected product and platform. Because the bonus is while supplies last, an old announcement alone is not enough at checkout.

Retailer and Platform Preorder Checklist

Natsume has named its store, Amazon, Target, Walmart, Best Buy, and GameStop, but a named retailer does not guarantee every platform, permanent stock, or identical bonus terms.

Price Tax Shipping Checklist

Price, Tax, Duty, and Shipping Checklist

Release Day Delivery Expectations

Release-Day Delivery Expectations

September 24, 2026 is the game’s release date, but the Natsume Store does not guarantee that a physical preorder arrives that day. Release timing and parcel delivery are separate events.

Combined Order Shipping Risk

Combined Preorder Shipping Risk

The Natsume Store says orders containing preorder and in-stock items are held until all items are available. Buyers who want an in-stock item sooner should place it separately.
