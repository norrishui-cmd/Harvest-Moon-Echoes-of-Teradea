Where to Buy Echoes of Teradea: Retailer Availability

A source-checked guide to the retailers Natsume has named for Echoes of Teradea preorders.

The distinction

Natsume has named its own store, Amazon, Target, Walmart, Best Buy, and GameStop. The May announcement specifically named Amazon for Switch 2, Switch, and PS5; the June trailer release added the other major retailers.

Confirmed side

01

A retailer name in a publisher release confirms announced availability, not permanent stock.

02

Each retailer can have different cancellation, delivery, pickup, and bonus terms.

03

The Natsume Store describes its wolf plush as an exclusive offer while supplies last.

Decision rule

Compare the exact platform variant, seller identity, delivery estimate, and displayed bonus before paying. Save the order confirmation if a limited bonus matters.

Known boundary

This page does not claim that every retailer carries every platform or includes the same bonus.
