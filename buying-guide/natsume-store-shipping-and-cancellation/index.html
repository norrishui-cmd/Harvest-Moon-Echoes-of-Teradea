Natsume Store Preorder Shipping and Cancellation Rules

The first-party store rules that matter before ordering Echoes of Teradea, including combined shipments and cancellation limits.

Policy summary

The Natsume Store says most preorders ship a few days before release, does not guarantee release-day delivery, holds mixed preorder and in-stock orders until everything is available, and limits preorder cancellation after the stated early window.

Terms that affect the order

01

The product is classified as a Q3 preorder, covering July through September.

02

International customers may face customs fees, duties, or taxes.

03

The live store policy, checkout terms, and support response control an individual order.

Buyer action

Place in-stock products separately if you want them earlier, verify the address, and read the current cancellation language before checkout rather than relying only on this summary.

Known boundary

This guide cannot predict a carrier, exact ship date, customs charge, transit delay, or release-day arrival.
