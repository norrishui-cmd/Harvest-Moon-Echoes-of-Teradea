Lupo Wolf Plush Preorder Bonus Eligibility Checklist

How to verify whether an Echoes of Teradea preorder includes the Lupo baby wolf plush.

Quick check

Lupo is the baby wolf plush identified as the Bloomfield Guardian. Natsume promotes it as a preorder bonus while supplies last, but buyers should confirm the exact retailer listing and selected product before assuming eligibility.

Verification steps

01

The May and June announcements connect Lupo to the preorder campaign.

02

The Natsume Store calls the wolf plush an exclusive offer and displays eligible physical platform variants.

03

“While supplies last” means availability can end before release.

Before you rely on the claim

Look for Lupo in the live product title or bonus description, verify the platform, avoid relying on an old screenshot, and retain proof that the offer appeared when ordering.

Known boundary

The plush is merchandise, not a confirmed in-game item, pet unlock, DLC code, or gameplay bonus.
