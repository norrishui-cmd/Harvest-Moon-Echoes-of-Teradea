International Import and Region Checklist

What international buyers should check before importing the Natsume Store physical edition of Echoes of Teradea.

Quick check

The Natsume Store states that its video games are NTSC products for the US, Canada, and Mexico region and tells international buyers to verify compatibility. Customs, duties, taxes, delivery, and platform-account compatibility require separate checks.

Verification steps

01

Import fees are not included in the publisher’s headline product price.

02

Undeliverable orders caused by unpaid customs, incorrect addresses, or abandonment may not be refunded.

03

Game or DLC incompatibility is not eligible for returns under the displayed international notice.

Before you rely on the claim

Confirm console compatibility, account region, delivery address, import charges, and return exposure before ordering. Recheck the final box and platform listing closer to launch.

Known boundary

The store notice does not publish a complete language list or guarantee compatibility for every country and account setup.
