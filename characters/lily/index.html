Lily Character Profile

What a visible quest objective confirms about Lily, favorite treats, Rick, and Bloomfield Park.

Quick answer

Lily is a named character in a visible objective asking the player to obtain her favorite treats after a request from Rick.

How this connects to the game

Lily’s page has a concrete relationship-and-quest anchor that can later support gift and request data. Until the item, quantity, trigger, and reward are verified, it remains an evidence profile rather than a walkthrough.
