Remote Island Expedition Loop

How nautical charts, remote islands, treasure hunting, and rare animals connect in Echoes of Teradea.

Planning answer

Nautical charts are used to locate remote islands. Those islands support treasure hunting and encounters with rare animals that are not found on the mainland.

Connected systems

01

Island discovery is presented as a distinct exploration activity.

02

Rare animals can be befriended, not merely observed.

03

Treasures and collectibles give island trips a resource purpose beyond sightseeing.

Launch-data upgrade path

A launch-ready island page should later record the chart source, departure point, route, prerequisites, island resources, animal availability, and return conditions for each verified island.

Known boundary

No island names, chart locations, boat mechanics, weather restrictions, or treasure tables are officially available yet.
