Maze-Like Cave and Mining Expedition Guide

What is confirmed about caves, ore, gems, obstacles, stamina, and preparation for mining trips.

Planning answer

Teradea contains maze-like caves with ore and gems. Movement skills, animal obstacle abilities, stamina recovery, and campsite cooking form the confirmed toolset that may support longer mining expeditions.

Connected systems

01

Quarrytop is the named mining village and is affected by earthquakes.

02

Caves are part of the massive open-world exploration description.

03

Ore and gems are confirmed resources, but their individual names and uses are not yet listed.

Launch-data upgrade path

After launch, cave guides should separate entrances, floors, hazards, resource nodes, shortcuts, required companions, rewards, and safe return routes instead of producing one shallow page per unknown ore.

Known boundary

Exact cave maps, mine depth, tool requirements, drop rates, sell prices, and respawn rules remain unknown.
