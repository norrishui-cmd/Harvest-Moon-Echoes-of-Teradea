Animal Companion Abilities and Obstacle Planning

A system guide to matching animal companions with hidden areas, rocks, fallen trees, jumps, and other exploration barriers.

System answer

Animal companions and mounts have special exploration abilities. Natsume specifically says animals can help reach hidden areas, break rocks and fallen trees, leap across terrain, and reveal treasures.

Confirmed mechanics

01

The companion system goes beyond cosmetic following.

02

Player movement and animal abilities are designed to be combined.

03

Rare animals on remote islands expand the exploration and befriending loop.

Future database structure

The eventual launch database should organize companions by obstacle solved, traversal role, acquisition requirement, and whether the same barrier can be handled by player movement.

Known boundary

Specific animal names, ability values, cooldowns, unlock quests, and a complete obstacle list are not yet published.
