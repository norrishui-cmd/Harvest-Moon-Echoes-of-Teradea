Does a Five-Platform Launch Confirm Cross-Buy?

No. The announcements identify supported platforms but do not state that one purchase unlocks another platform version.

Quick answer

No. The announcements identify supported platforms but do not state that one purchase unlocks another platform version.

Confirmed evidence

  • Nintendo, PlayStation, Xbox, and Steam are separate ecosystems
  • physical selectors are separate products
  • no cross-buy language appears.
  • Primary source: Natsume title announcement, dated 2026-03-11.

How to use this answer

Assume each selected version is a separate purchase unless an official listing says otherwise.

1

Check the dated first-party source.

2

Separate the confirmed fact from platform, retailer, or story assumptions.

3

Recheck the live listing before acting on changing data.

What this does not confirm

Cross-buy, upgrade, and save-transfer policies require explicit storefront terms.

Source and review date

Natsume title announcement · 2026-03-11. Editorial review: 2026-07-31.
