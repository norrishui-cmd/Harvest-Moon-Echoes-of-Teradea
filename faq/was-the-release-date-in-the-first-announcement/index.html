Was the Release Date Included in the First Announcement?

No. Natsume revealed the game on March 11, 2026 without a launch date; September 24, 2026 was announced with preorders in May. Source-checked status, evidence boundaries, and related player guidance.

Quick answer

No. Natsume revealed the game on March 11, 2026 without a launch date; September 24, 2026 was announced with preorders in May.

Evidence checked

  • The March reveal confirmed the title, platforms and major systems.
  • The May preorder announcement supplied the September 24 date.
  • The June trailer announcement repeated that date.

What this does—and does not—confirm

This separates the reveal date from the release-date announcement and avoids treating early coverage as a delay report. Exact store unlock hours still require platform data.

This answer covers Harvest Moon: Echoes of Teradea only. It does not copy schedules, gifts, values, controls or mechanics from earlier Harvest Moon games.

Evidence source

Official publisher announcement: Natsume preorder announcement — May 12, 2026. Independently summarized and checked on 2026-07-26.
