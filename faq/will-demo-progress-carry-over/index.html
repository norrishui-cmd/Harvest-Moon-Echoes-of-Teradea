Will Demo Progress Carry Over to the Full Game?

There are no save-transfer rules because no public demo has been announced. Source-checked status, evidence boundaries, and related player guidance.

Quick answer

There are no save-transfer rules because no public demo has been announced.

Evidence checked

  • No verified demo currently creates transferable progress.
  • The trailer does not describe a trial-save system.
  • No preorder reward is tied to demo completion.

What this does—and does not—confirm

Farm, character, item and settings carry-over claims are unsupported until Natsume publishes a demo and its rules.

This answer covers Harvest Moon: Echoes of Teradea only. It does not copy schedules, gifts, values, controls or mechanics from earlier Harvest Moon games.

Evidence source

Official publisher announcement: Natsume first-trailer announcement — June 18, 2026. Independently summarized and checked on 2026-07-26.
