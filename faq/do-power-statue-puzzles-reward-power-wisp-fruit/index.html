Do Power Statue Puzzles Reward Power Wisp Fruit?

Yes. Natsume connects solved Power Statue puzzles with Power Wisp Fruit, which is used to increase maximum stamina.

Quick answer

Evidence checked

  • Their puzzles award Power Wisp Fruit.
