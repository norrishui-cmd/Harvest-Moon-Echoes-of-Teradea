Is Echoes of Teradea a Story of Seasons Game?

No. Echoes of Teradea is a Natsume-developed and published Harvest Moon game, not a Story of Seasons title. Source-checked status, evidence boundaries, and related player guidance.

Quick answer

No. Echoes of Teradea is a Natsume-developed and published Harvest Moon game, not a Story of Seasons title.

Evidence checked

  • Natsume is the named developer and publisher.
  • The official title uses the Harvest Moon brand.
  • Story of Seasons is a separate modern series.

What this does—and does not—confirm

Similar farming-life themes do not make the games part of the same current franchise or guarantee shared systems.

This answer covers Harvest Moon: Echoes of Teradea only. It does not copy schedules, gifts, values, controls or mechanics from earlier Harvest Moon games.

Evidence source

Official publisher announcement: Natsume title announcement — March 11, 2026. Independently summarized and checked on 2026-07-26.
