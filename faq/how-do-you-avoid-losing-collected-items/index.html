How Do You Avoid Losing Collected Items in the Wilderness?

Natsume says players must evade or escape wolves, bears and tigers; being caught causes collected items to be lost and sends the player back to the farm. Source-checked status, evidence boundaries, and related player guidance.

Quick answer

Natsume says players must evade or escape wolves, bears and tigers; being caught causes collected items to be lost and sends the player back to the farm.

Evidence checked

  • Wild animals are active threats.
  • The consequence is item loss plus return to the farm.
  • Companions and expanded movement support exploration.

What this does—and does not—confirm

Exact stealth, sprint, stamina, safe-zone and recovery mechanics need verified gameplay before a step-by-step route can be published.

This answer covers Harvest Moon: Echoes of Teradea only. It does not copy schedules, gifts, values, controls or mechanics from earlier Harvest Moon games.

Evidence source

Official publisher announcement: Natsume first-trailer announcement — June 18, 2026. Independently summarized and checked on 2026-07-26.
