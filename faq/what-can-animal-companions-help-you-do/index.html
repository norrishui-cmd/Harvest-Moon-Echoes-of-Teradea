What Can Animal Companions Help You Do?

Animal companions can cross terrain, break rocks and fallen trees, reach hidden areas and uncover valuable treasures. Source-checked status, evidence boundaries, and related player guidance.

Quick answer

Animal companions can cross terrain, break rocks and fallen trees, reach hidden areas and uncover valuable treasures.

Evidence checked

  • Different companions have different abilities.
  • Pets, animals and mounts can join adventures.
