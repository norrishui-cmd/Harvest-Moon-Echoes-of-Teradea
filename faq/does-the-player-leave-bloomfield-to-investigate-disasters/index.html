Does the Player Leave Bloomfield to Investigate Disasters?

Yes. The official premise begins in Bloomfield and sends the player across Teradea as mist, wild beasts, and disasters threaten the land.

Quick answer

Evidence checked

  • The protagonist was raised in Bloomfield Village.
  • The Forest of Echoes mist and nighttime wolves disrupt the region.
  • Other villages face earthquakes, violent storms, and additional disasters.
