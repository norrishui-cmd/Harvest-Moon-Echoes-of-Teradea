Bloomfield Wolves Connection

Bloomfield, Night Wolves, and the Opening Threat

Bloomfield Village is the protagonist’s home, and the spreading Forest of Echoes mist brings wild wolves out at night. That connection establishes the local threat that starts the wider journey.

Forest Mist Guardian Spirits

Forest Mist and Guardian Spirits Connection

The mist spreading from the Forest of Echoes threatens Teradea, while helping Guardian Spirits revive the land becomes the larger mission. The official premise connects the forest mystery to supernatural restoration without revealing the resolution.

Tidewind Storm Restoration

Tidewind Storm and Restoration Connection

Tidewind is the port village where the player investigates violent storms. Its disaster gives the restoration journey a coastal and weather-focused branch.

Quarrytop Earthquake Mining

Quarrytop Earthquake and Mining Connection

Quarrytop is a mining village affected by earthquakes. The setting links a community disaster to the broader cave and ore landscape, but Natsume has not said that mining causes the earthquakes.

Maplehill Community Revival

Maplehill Community Revival Connection

Maplehill is a once-thriving town and cultural center whose light and community must be revived. Its branch emphasizes social and cultural restoration rather than a named storm or earthquake.

Guardian Wolf Vs Lupo

Guardian Wolf vs Lupo: What Is Actually Confirmed?

Official material mentions a rumored large guardian wolf and separately identifies Lupo as a baby wolf plush called the Bloomfield Guardian. The sources do not confirm that they are the same character or define an in-game relationship.

Four Village Disaster Map

Four-Village Disaster and Restoration Map

The confirmed world map of story purposes is: Bloomfield as home under the forest mist and wolf threat, Tidewind under storms, Quarrytop under earthquakes, and Maplehill in cultural decline.

Friendship Romance Restoration

Friendship, Romance, and Restoration Connection

Building friendships and choosing among ten love interests are confirmed life-sim systems, while helping villagers contributes to Teradea’s development. Official sources connect social play to the world but do not publish romance statistics or restoration bonuses.
